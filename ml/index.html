RLBalatro

A headless C++17 port of Balatro 1.0.1o verified action-by-action against the game's Lua code in LuaJIT 2.0.5 (0 mismatches across 5,950+ runs and ~446,000 actions), paired with a 6-layer Transformer PPO policy trained for 3.28B steps (13.56M games) on Gold Stake across all 15 decks.

Gold Stake Joker Stats

Win rate vs. most blinds played.

Note on reward shaping: Earlier runs that included a small reward for holding cash (--reward-money 1) mostly played Pairs and hoarded $500+ for Bull and Bootstraps (peaking around 6.38M chips). Dropping the money reward (--reward-money 0) got the agent to spend on rerolls, booster packs, and scaling xMult jokers (Driver's License, Steel Joker, Vampire, Constellation, Hologram), pushing peak Gold Stake runs over 11.38B chips.
